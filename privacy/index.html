1. Who we are

Beats By Sir Edi is a mobile app for streaming and licensing instrumental beats. It is run by Motloang Mohale, trading as Beats By Sir Edi, based in Kenya ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: siredibeatz@gmail.com.

This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. By creating an account you confirm you have read it.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to run your account and your purchases. We do not use advertising, ad trackers or third-party analytics.

Data Examples Where it comes from
Account details Your name, email address, password You, when you sign up. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
Library and preferences Favourite beats, playlists you create You, as you use the app
Orders Beat and license bought, price in KSh, payment method type (M-Pesa or card), payment reference, date, refund status Created when a payment succeeds
Payment details M-Pesa phone number, card details You enter these on Paystack's secure checkout page. We never receive your full card number, PIN or M-Pesa PIN. Paystack may share limited details with us, such as the phone number used or the last four digits of a card.
Technical data Sign-in tokens, error logs, IP address in server logs Your device and our service providers, automatically
On your device only Volume setting, cached audio previews, downloaded beats Stored on your phone; not sent to us

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera or microphone. The app asks for access to your photos only if you are the store administrator uploading cover art.

3. How we use your data

Purpose Legal basis (Data Protection Act, 2019)

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or to build marketing profiles. If we ever want to send you marketing messages, we will ask for your consent first, and you can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers that run the app, and only what each one needs. Each processes it under its own privacy terms.

Provider What they do for us Data involved
Google Firebase (Google LLC) Sign-in and the app database (accounts, favourites, playlists, orders) Account details, library, orders
Supabase, Inc. Stores beat audio and cover art; runs our payment server Sign-in token (user ID and email) and order details while a payment is processed

We may also disclose data if Kenyan law requires it, for example to a court or the Kenya Revenue Authority, or to protect our rights in a dispute. If the business is ever sold or transferred, customer data may pass to the new owner under this policy.

5. Transfers, retention and security

Transfers outside Kenya. Our providers store data on servers outside Kenya. The app database is hosted by Google in South Africa (Johannesburg region); other providers may process data in the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States. We rely on these providers' contractual and security safeguards, as the Data Protection Act requires.

How long we keep data.

Data Kept for
Account, favourites, playlists Until you delete your account
Orders and payment records 5 years after the purchase, for tax and accounting law
Server and error logs Up to 90 days

Security. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules limit each user to their own data, and only the store administrator can change the catalogue or see all orders. Payments are handled by Paystack, which is PCI DSS certified. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can:

  • Be told how your data is used (this policy)
  • Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Have incorrect data corrected
  • Have your data deleted, including your whole account
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw any consent you have given

To use any of these rights, including deleting your account, email siredibeatz@gmail.com from your account's email address. We will reply within 14 days. Deleting your account removes your profile, favourites and playlists. Order records are kept for the period in section 5, because the law requires it.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

7. Children

The app is meant for people aged 18 and over. Anyone under 18 may use it only with a parent's or guardian's consent, and purchases must be made by an adult. We do not knowingly collect data from children without that consent. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
