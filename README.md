## About the app

- **Stream every beat:** preview the full catalogue, build playlists and save favourites. It plays in the background and on the lock screen.
- **Clear licenses:** MP3 Lease, WAV Lease or Trackout Exclusive, with simple limits you can read before you buy.
- **Pay with M-Pesa or card:** secure checkout by Paystack, priced in Kenyan shillings.
- **Instant downloads:** beats land in your Library straight away, with a PDF license certificate for your records.

## How to install

1. On your Android phone, tap **[Download for Android](https://github.com/siredibeatz/beatsbysiredi/releases/latest/download/Beats-By-Sir-Edi.apk)**.
2. Open **Beats-By-Sir-Edi.apk** from your notifications or Downloads.
3. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
4. Open **Beats By Sir Edi**, create your account and start listening.

**Updating:** install the new version over the old one. Your account and purchases stay the same.
