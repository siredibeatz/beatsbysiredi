Delete your account

Beats By Sir Edi app · developer: Motloang Mohale

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Beats By Sir Edi app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Library tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Or ask us by email

Can't open the app? Email siredibeatz@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 14 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Sign-in account (email, password)Deleted immediately
Profile, favourites, playlistsDeleted immediately
Purchase records (beat, license, amount, payment reference)Kept for 5 years after purchase, as tax and accounting law requires, then deleted
Beats you downloadedStay on your phone. Your licenses remain valid under their terms
