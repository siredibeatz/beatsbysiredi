2. Terms for every license

Parties. The Producer is Motloang Mohale, professionally known as Sir Edi, trading as Beats By Sir Edi (Kenya, siredibeatz@gmail.com). The Licensee is the account holder who bought the license.

What you get. The Producer grants you the right to use the beat (the instrumental recording and its underlying music) to create one new song. You may add vocals and lyrics, and arrange or edit the beat for that song. Your rights are limited to the license you bought, as set out in section 1 and in sections 3 or 4. The license covers one song. Each further song needs its own license.

Credit. Every release, video and listing must credit the Producer as "Prod. by Sir Edi", in the title or the credits wherever the platform allows.

Producer tag. MP3 Lease and WAV Lease files include the Producer's audio tag, and the tag must stay in your song as delivered. You may not remove, mute, cover or edit it out. Trackout Exclusive buyers receive untagged files.

Ownership and publishing.

  • The Producer keeps the copyright in the beat and its composition.
  • You own the master recording of your new song, and your own lyrics and vocals.
  • The composition of the new song is shared 50% Producer / 50% you (the writers' and publishers' share combined). Each party registers and collects its own share with its collecting society, such as MCSK in Kenya.
  • The Producer does not take a share of your master royalties (streaming, sales or video income), only the publishing share above.

You may not:

  • Sell, share, lease or give away the beat itself, with or without changes, or release it as an instrumental
  • Register the beat, or a song using it, with YouTube Content ID or a similar fingerprinting system (leases); exclusive holders may, if they whitelist the Producer (section 4)
  • Claim you made the beat, or remove the Producer's credit
  • Use the beat in anything defamatory, hateful or otherwise unlawful

Samples. Beats contain no uncleared samples unless the beat's listing says so. You are responsible for clearing anything you add.

3. MP3 Lease and WAV Lease

Non-exclusive. The Producer may keep licensing the same beat to other artists, and may later sell it exclusively. An exclusive sale does not cancel your lease: you keep your rights until your limits are reached.

Limits. The stream, sales, video, radio and performance limits in section 1 apply across all platforms combined, for example Spotify, Apple Music, Boomplay, Audiomack and YouTube. Once your song reaches any limit, the lease is used up. You must upgrade, or stop further distribution of the song.

MP3 Lease. Delivered as a high-quality MP3 that includes the producer tag. Suited to mixtapes, demos, social media and first releases. Live performance only at shows where you are not paid to perform.

WAV Lease. Delivered as WAV and MP3, both including the producer tag. Suited to commercial singles and EPs, with one monetised music video, play on up to two radio stations, and paid live performances.

Not included in either lease: use in films, TV, adverts, video games or other sync, and Content ID registration.

4. Trackout Exclusive

Exclusive rights. From the date of purchase, you are the only person the Producer will license the beat to. The beat is removed from the store, and the Producer will not license, sell or release it again. The Producer keeps the copyright, the credit and the 50% publishing share in section 2.

Earlier leases. Artists who leased the beat before your purchase keep their lease rights until their limits are reached. The Producer will tell you, on request, whether the beat was leased before.

Unlimited use. No limits on streams, sales, videos, radio or live performances. Sync use in films, TV, adverts and games is allowed.

Files. The tagged preview is available in the app straight away. Untagged WAV and MP3 files and the track-outs (separate stems for each instrument) are sent to your account email within 3 working days of purchase.

Content ID. You may register your song with YouTube Content ID or similar systems. You must whitelist the Producer's own channels, and any earlier leaseholders, so their uses are not claimed.

Duration. The exclusive rights last for the full term of copyright, in every country.

5. General terms

Duration. Leases have no time limit. They last until your song reaches one of the limits in section 1. The exclusive lasts for the full term of copyright.

Breach. If you break this agreement and do not fix it within 14 days of being told, your license ends. You must then stop distributing the song, and the Producer may issue takedown notices to platforms. Refunds follow the Refund Policy in the Terms of Use.

Producer's promises. The Producer confirms that he created the beat and has the right to license it, and that it contains no uncleared samples unless the listing says so.

Your promises. You confirm that your additions to the song, such as lyrics, vocals and samples, do not infringe anyone's rights. You agree to cover the Producer's losses if they do.

Transfer. You may not transfer or sublicense this license, except to a record label or distributor that releases the song on your behalf under these same terms.

Whole agreement. This agreement, the Terms of Use and your order record in the app form the whole agreement for the license. If they conflict, this agreement wins on license rights. Changes must be agreed in writing, and email counts.

Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya, including the Copyright Act, 2001. Disputes go to the courts of Kenya, after both sides first try to settle them by email within 30 days.

Acceptance. By completing payment for a license in the Beats By Sir Edi app, you accept this agreement for that beat and license type, effective on the date of payment.
