Licenses

Pick the right license

NON-EXCLUSIVE
MP3 Lease

For mixtapes, demos and first releases.

  • MP3 with producer tag
  • Up to 50,000 streams
  • Up to 2,000 paid downloads
  • 1 music video (not monetised)
MOST POPULAR
WAV Lease

For commercial singles and EPs.

  • WAV + MP3 with producer tag
  • Up to 500,000 streams
  • Up to 10,000 paid downloads
  • 1 monetised video, 2 radio stations, paid shows
EXCLUSIVE
Trackout Exclusive

The beat is yours alone.

  • Untagged WAV + MP3 + stems
  • Unlimited streams, sales, videos and radio
  • Film, TV, ads and games (sync)
  • Removed from the store for everyone else
